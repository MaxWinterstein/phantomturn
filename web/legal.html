What this is

phantomturn is a private, non-commercial hobby project, provided free of charge and carrying no advertising.

Data

None is collected. Files you open are read and rewritten entirely in your browser and are never transmitted anywhere — there is no server component, no analytics, no cookies, and no third-party requests. The page's content security policy prevents outbound connections, so this is enforced by your browser rather than merely promised here.

The tool rewrites your activity files on a best-effort basis and is offered without warranty of any kind. Keep your original files. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Garmin.
