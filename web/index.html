🌊 phantomturn

Your watch counted turns that never happened. This puts the numbers back.

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser — the file never leaves this page.

🧪 A living experiment, and 100% vibe coded. Every line here was written by an AI — I haven't read any of it, and that's rather the point. I keep swimming and keep feeding it new files, so the defaults will keep moving.

A test suite and byte-exact comparison against a separate implementation stand in for a human having reviewed it. That isn't the same thing. Keep your original file.

⚙️ Assumptions

Every default here was calibrated on one swimmer, a Forerunner 265 and a 50 m pool. If your numbers come out wrong, this is where to fix them.

🕶️ Sharing this file?

A Garmin export carries your name, a biometric profile, your watch's serial number, a per-second heart-rate trace and the exact date you swam. This keeps only what a swim analysis reads and throws the rest away — durations, distances and stroke counts stay exact, so the file still reproduces the same problem.
