⚙️ Assumptions

Every default here was calibrated on one swimmer, a Forerunner 265 and a 50 m pool. If your numbers come out wrong, this is where to fix them.

How many pool lengths one press of the lap button covers. Anything beyond this in a single lap is treated as a phantom turn and merged away. Set it high — or to your interval length — if you don't lap once per length.
Depends on your stroke length and the pool. Roughly halve it for a 25 m pool.
A cross-check on the stroke count. When the two disagree the lap is reported as ambiguous and the watch's own label is kept.
Overrides the watch's stroke classification with the thresholds above.
Drops the gap between stopping the timer and saving. Only applied when the timer was never paused.

🕶️ Sharing this file?

A Garmin export carries your name, a biometric profile, your watch's serial number, a per-second heart-rate trace and the exact date you swam. This keeps only what a swim analysis reads and throws the rest away — durations, distances and stroke counts stay exact, so the file still reproduces the same problem.
